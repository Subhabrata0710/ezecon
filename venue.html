AltAir Boutique Hotel, Kolkata

AltAir Boutique Hotel

Located in the vibrant IT corridor of Sector V, Salt Lake City, AltAir Boutique Hotel offers a modern, premium setting perfect for an international medical conference. The hotel features contemporary conference facilities, excellent dining, and is well-connected to all major transport hubs.

📍 Address: EM 4, EM Block, Sector V, Salt Lake City, Kolkata – 700091
✈️ Airport: NSCBI Airport — ~11.5 km (~25 min)
🚂 Sealdah Station: ~8.5 km (~30 min)
🚂 Howrah Station: ~12 km (~45 min)
🚇 Nearest Metro: Salt Lake Sector V (Aqua Line)

How to Reach

✈️

By Air

Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport is approximately 11.5 km away. Pre-paid taxis and app-based cabs (Ola/Uber) are readily available.

🚂

By Train

Sealdah Station: ~8.5 km (30 min)
Howrah Station: ~12 km (45 min)
Both stations are well connected via metro and cabs.

🚇

By Metro & Road

Salt Lake Sector V Metro (East-West Aqua Line) is the nearest metro station. Buses, auto-rickshaws, and app cabs are available across the city.
