Oral Presentation Submission – EZECON 2026

EZECON 2026 invites abstract submissions for Oral Presentations from trainees, residents, registrars, Medical Officers, and young clinicians in Emergency Medicine, Critical Care, Trauma, and allied acute care specialties.

The Oral Presentation session is designed to provide a scientific platform for presenting original research, case reports, case series, audits, quality improvement projects, and clinically relevant academic work related to emergency and acute care medicine.

All submitted abstracts will undergo review by the Scientific Committee. Selected abstracts will be presented as slide-based oral presentations during EZECON 2026. There will be no poster or e-poster category for this section.
