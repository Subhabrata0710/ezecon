Welcome to EZECON 2026

A premier academic summit bringing together leading experts in Emergency Medicine

We are delighted to invite you to the 5th East Zone Emergency Medicine Conference (EZECON) 2026 & the 8th Bengal EM Conclave, to be held on 8th & 9th August 2026 at AltAir Boutique Hotel, Salt Lake City, Kolkata.

Organized by the Society for Emergency Medicine India (SEMI) — West Bengal Chapter, EZECON 2026 promises an outstanding academic programme featuring national faculty, workshops, poster presentations, and interactive case-based sessions.

The conference theme — "Where Knowledge Meets Compassion!" — reflects our commitment to combining clinical excellence with compassionate patient care.
